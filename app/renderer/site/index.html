How it works

  1. 1

    Set up once.

    We learn your experience and your voice.

  2. 2

    We search.

    Matched jobs and tailored materials, in your voice.

  3. 3

    You approve.

    Nothing goes out without your yes.

  • Straight about fit

    Every match is marked strong, good or stretch, with a plain reason. Weak ones are skipped and we say why.

  • Honest keywords

    Keywords from the job description, only where your experience backs them up.

  • Interview prep in your voice

    A one-page prep from your own stories and the job, so you walk in ready to tell them your way.

  • Every job, tracked

    Every job and every resume and letter you made for it, in one place. Never double-apply.

  • Your design, or ours

    Keep your current resume’s look, or pick one of eight clean templates and make it yours.

  • Learns from your edits

    Every yes, no and edit teaches us. The rules we learn are in plain words, and you can turn any of them off.
